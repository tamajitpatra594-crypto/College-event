About CSE (AIML)

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at the Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata, offers a forward-looking four-year B.Tech programme immersed in machine intelligence, deep learning, natural language processing, and advanced robotics.

Equipped with state-of-the-art AIML and IoT research laboratories, the department collaborates vigorously with industry leaders, the IETE Student Forum, and the IEM Innovation Cell. Through Eventrix, we nurture real-world engineering excellence, creative collaboration, and student leadership.
